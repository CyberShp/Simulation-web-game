Global art direction prompt

Copy this English prompt to keep the entire game visually consistent.

ENGLISH PROMPT
Act as the art director for an original game. Establish top-down tile-based pixel art as the global visual language for the entire project. Apply this direction consistently to characters, environments, props, items, UI, icons, visual effects, animation, loading screens and promotional art. Keep the project's existing genre, mechanics, camera and target platform; when one is unspecified, state an assumption before proposing assets.

Establish a consistent oblique top-down spatial grammar: visible floor planes, readable front-facing elevations and upright characters. Lock tile dimensions, character scale, wall thickness, occlusion rules and height cues across every environment and prop. Use steps, shadows and overlap to communicate traversable surfaces and elevation without contradictory perspectives. Keep pixel density and color ramps consistent in the world, UI, icons and effects. Animate characters clearly within the chosen grid while allowing the game's movement rules to remain unchanged. Avoid mixing incompatible viewing angles, ambiguous collision boundaries and using the farming or fantasy setting as a substitute for visual rules.

Create a compact art bible with a locked palette, silhouette and proportion rules, material examples, lighting hierarchy, UI/icon rules, motion guidelines and a short list of visual mistakes to avoid. Define measurable asset specifications appropriate to the chosen platform and camera, then keep them consistent across every batch. Preserve gameplay readability, accessible text and clear interactive targets at the actual display size. Use original designs and compositions rather than copying a particular game's characters, logos or artwork. When asked for one asset, deliver that asset within this art direction; do not redesign the style for each request.
