Scale is crucial: exactly TWO visible adult residents, one blue/teal-robed master standing near the main hall entrance/front court, and one lavender/cream-robed woman working at the herb plot. Adults approximately 1.8m; door approximately 2.6m, human height about 70% of door opening; main hall ground facade about 8–10m wide and significantly larger than a person. Ground slab width about 0.5m, NOT giant slabs. Herb plot wide enough for several rows and a human worker, about twice a person's standing height across. Do not miniaturize the people to fit a huge scene.
Lighting: soft late-morning light from upper-left, cool blue-green ambient shade beneath eaves and pine canopy, warm stone midtones, readable local contact shadows. Fine texture variation and dense handcrafted detail concentrated in focal architecture/trees/rocks, calm traversable paths.
Constraints: one fully composed scene, no UI, no labels, no lettering, no inset panels, no grid, no diagram, no open building cutaways. Pure finely resolved 2D pixel game art, not a smooth 3D render or coarse low-resolution sprite collage. No repeated checkerboard lawn, no giant featureless rectangular plaza.
