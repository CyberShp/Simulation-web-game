# 生成记录

工具：内置 `image_gen.imagegen`，编辑模式，非透明背景。输入为会话中的同视角比例参考；输出为本目录 `prototype.png`。最终提示词如下：

> Use case: style-transfer. Asset type: playable Chinese xianxia sect-management game scene visual prototype. Input image is the exact composition and scale reference. Transform the ENTIRE image into a CLEAR, CALM, MATURE CHINESE FLAT-COLOR ILLUSTRATED GAME ART style (清爽国风平涂). This must be a decisive stylistic transformation, not merely reducing detail on the same realistic painting. Preserve the existing fixed oblique camera and relative layout: substantial main hall in upper right, broad open courtyard in middle, cultivated herb beds in lower left, ancient framing pine to upper left, winding path and cloud mountains in distance. Keep three outdoor adult people: two clearly separated in the forecourt and one working beside the herb beds. Main hall remains large and the architectural focal point, harmonious adult people and door proportions. VISUAL LANGUAGE: broad clean hand-shaped color masses; restrained elegant dark colored contours on buildings and people; one lit plane and one softly contrasting shadow plane per major form; flat opaque colors with at most extremely subtle paper grain. Simplify mountains into broad pale blue silhouette layers and ample soft cloud bands. Consolidate foliage into a few large overlapping celadon/jade-green clusters, rocks into calm muted blue-gray angular masses, paths into wide clean warm-beige surfaces. Leave most courtyard surface untextured. Sparse deliberately placed tufts, no scatter of repeated tiny marks. Roof should be big readable indigo planes, with only a few meaningful ridge lines, simplified gold end ornaments and restrained timber geometry, not hundreds of tiles. Windows and doors read as simple clear shapes. Stairs readable broad treads, clean integrated foundation. Herb garden has four or five gently angled dark earth rows, each carrying a handful of simple herb clumps; integrated low earth borders matching surrounding ground, coherent path connection. People use strong readable silhouettes and distinct simple warm ochre/ivory/teal clothing areas, clean foot-contact shadows, consistent human anatomy; they should remain easy to see when the whole composition is reduced to game-screen size. Mood: serene, airy, tasteful xianxia, polished 2D strategy/management game background inspired by refined Chinese animated film color design. Palette: muted jade and celadon, slate/indigo, warm ivory and pale sand, tiny restrained ochre accents. Lower contrast and lower detail for scenery, slightly stronger contrast for people and architecture. Camera and geometric depth remain coherent. No UI, no text, no frames. Avoid photorealism, 3D render materials, glossy surfaces, intricate stippling, leaf-by-leaf painting, gravel noise, tiny white flower specks, dense decorations, busy roof textures, chibi bodies, thick cartoon outlines, gradients everywhere, extra buildings or extra people. Output one whole game-scene prototype, same nearly square composition as reference.
