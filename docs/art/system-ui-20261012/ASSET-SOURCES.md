> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent 2D game user-interface window frame, for a Chinese xianxia cultivation management PC game. A single landscape rectangular frame, front view, perfectly straight aligned geometry, thin dark desaturated jade-green lacquered wood rim, subtle hand painted warm bronze inlay with cloud-scroll motifs, small carved traditional auspicious cloud corner brackets, restrained crafted Chinese fantasy RPG inventory panel quality. Entire central area is genuinely transparent, empty, about 90% of image area. Only the rim and four corner brackets are opaque, no backing parchment, no scene, no writing, no icons, no buttons. Flat orthographic view, painterly realistic materials, subtle worn jade grain and warm edge highlights, little shadow on the inner edge only. Frame comes close to canvas edges with a tiny transparent exterior margin. Landscape 3:2 composition. Top center has a small cloud-shaped bronze ornament, bottom center a tiny jade knot decoration. Do not make a scroll with rollers. The border must remain slender to maximize usable game interface space. This is a reusable game UI border art asset with alpha transparency, not a complete menu screenshot.
